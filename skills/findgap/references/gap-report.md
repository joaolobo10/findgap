# Gap report

How to turn a diagnosis into a report the learner can act on: what they really know, what they only thought they knew, and what to do next.

## When

- **End of a full diagnosis:** the full report.
- **End of a quick check:** the short report (headline, dangerous gaps, next step).
- **On request,** at any point: report on what has been tested so far, and say clearly what hasn't.

## Principles

- **Dangerous gaps first.** They matter most and are easiest to miss in a long report.
- **Evidence, not impressions.** Every claim points to what the learner did: an answer, a rung reached, a confidence rating.
- **Kind and direct.** Name every gap plainly, without softening it into vagueness and without making the learner feel bad. Exam-board mode: same content, terser and stricter wording, never insulting.
- **Plain words.** Say "you could explain it but not use it", not "stalled at rung 4". Use the learner's language for statuses and gap types.
- **Short.** A quick-check report fits on one screen. A full report should be readable in about two minutes; put detail in tables, not paragraphs.
- **Honest about limits.** Say what was not tested, and that questions written by an AI can contain errors.

## Structure of the full report

### 1. Headline

The readiness verdict and one sentence of why (see the readiness rubric). If there is not enough evidence for a verdict, say what is missing instead.

### 2. Dangerous gaps

Every wrong answer given with high confidence (3–4), most critical first (rated 4, then high-weight and safety-critical topics). For each:

- **Topic.**
- **What you believed** (their answer, briefly).
- **What is actually true**, in 1–2 sentences.
- **The misconception behind it**, if one was identified.
- **Re-test date.**

If there are none, say so. That is a genuinely good sign.

### 3. Map status

One table covering every topic tested:

| Topic | Status | Reached / target | What it means | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1.1 … | dangerous gap | Explain / Analyze | You felt sure, but the core idea was off | Correct the misconception, re-test on [date] |
| T2.1 … | fragile | Apply / Apply | You got there, but weren't sure and needed a hint | More practice recalling it without help |
| T3.2 … | mastered | Discriminate / Analyze | Solid; confirm it later | Re-test on [date] |

List untested topics separately, with the share of the map's weight they represent.

### 4. How well you judged yourself

Use the calibration summary rules, and only with at least 8 rated items. Also include:

- the share of answers in each of the four matrix cells;
- topics where the self-rating dropped after explaining (illusion of explanatory depth).

Describe the pattern, never label the person.

### 5. Your materials

- Topics the criterion requires that the learner's materials don't cover.
- Material they own but said they didn't actually study, when it matters.

### 6. What is solid

Name specific topics that are mastered or confirmed, and any underconfidence: "you know more than you think about X". Keep it real and specific, never generic praise.

### 7. Next steps

The 3 highest-priority actions, in order, each tied to a gap. Then hand off to the roadmap.

### 8. Limits of this diagnosis

One or two lines:

- what share of the map was tested, and how many items;
- that the questions were generated by an AI. In high-stakes fields, add a reminder to verify key facts against the official criterion or source;
- that findgap diagnoses but does not certify.

## The short report (quick check)

1. Headline (a verdict only if the evidence allows it; otherwise "first impression").
2. Dangerous gaps, if any.
3. One line on what is solid.
4. One next step, plus an offer to run a full diagnosis.

## Saving the report

- **State is being kept:** update `gap-log.md` (topic statuses, dangerous gaps with re-test dates, readiness history, session history). In Claude Code, also save the report as `.findgap/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- **Claude app with a Project:** give the learner the updated `gap-log.md` and ask them to save it to the Project.
- **No state:** offer to give them the report as a document they can keep.
